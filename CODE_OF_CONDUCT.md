# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Phishing Forensics respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give and accept constructive feedback with good intent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Protect privacy: do not post real email content, personal data, credentials, or other sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or intimidation.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, private vulnerability details, or sensitive email data in public project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may limit participation when necessary to keep project spaces safe and constructive. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Do not use a public issue to report a private concern.
